# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2025_

This Privacy Policy describes how **Aeternum Sentinel** (“we”, “us”, or “our”)
collects, uses, and shares information when you use our software, services, and any
related tools (collectively, the “Service”).

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in
accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not access or use the
Service.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Information you provide**  
We may collect personal information you provide directly to us, such as account
details, configuration data, or other inputs that you submit when configuring or
using the Service.

**Information collected automatically**  
When you access or use the Service, we may automatically collect certain technical
information, including log files, device identifiers, IP address, operating system,
client version, and usage data related to feature interactions.

**Information from third parties**  
If the Service integrates with third-party platforms (e.g., Discord, Google
services), we may receive information from those third parties in accordance with
their own privacy policies and your settings with them.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

- provide, maintain, and improve the Service;
- personalize your experience;
- communicate with you, including responding to inquiries and providing support;
- monitor usage trends and enhance security; and
- comply with legal obligations.

We may aggregate or anonymize data for analytics, research, or other lawful purposes.

## 3. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may share information:

- with service providers and contractors who assist in operating the Service and are
  bound by confidentiality obligations;
- with third-party platforms you connect to the Service, subject to your
  authorization and their terms;
- to comply with legal obligations, enforce our Terms of Service, or protect our
  rights, property, or safety; and
- in connection with a merger, acquisition, or other corporate transaction, subject
  to appropriate safeguards.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes
described in this policy, and as required by law. We may retain anonymized or
aggregated data indefinitely.

## 5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information
against loss, theft, misuse, or unauthorized access. However, no method of
transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we
cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. International Transfers

If you access the Service from outside the jurisdiction in which it is hosted, your
information may be transferred, stored, and processed in that jurisdiction. By using
the Service, you consent to such transfers subject to applicable law.

## 7. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from children. If you become aware that a child has
provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate
steps.

## 8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding access, correction,
deletion, portability, or restriction of your personal information. To exercise
these rights, contact us using the information provided below. We may ask you to
verify your identity before responding.

If you receive communications from us, you may opt out by following any unsubscribe
instructions or contacting us directly.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this
page with a new “Last updated” date. Your continued use of the Service after changes
are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.

## 10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please reach
